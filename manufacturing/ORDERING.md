# JLCPCB order settings — Rev C

Upload `JLCPCB-Gerbers-RevC.zip` for the PCB, then `BOM.csv` and `CPL.csv` for top-side assembly.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Board | 100 × 60 mm, four copper layers, 1.6 mm FR-4 |
| Stack | Standard four-layer FR-4; 1 oz outer copper |
| Finish / colours | ENIG, black solder mask, white legend |
| Via process | Through vias, 0.30 mm drill / 0.55 mm pad, tented both sides |
| Drilling | Standard 0.30 mm through vias |
| Routing | 0.15 mm minimum traces; 0.15 mm routing clearance target, 0.10 mm fabrication floor |
| Outline | Routed chamfered outline with USB tongue; four 3.2 mm mounting holes |
| Assembly | Top side, 70 components; match the supplied LCSC/JLCPCB codes |
| Stencil | Top only, 0.10 mm recommended; `SMT-Stencil-RevC.zip` |

The router uses four copper layers; inner1 also has a ground pour. No blind, buried or filled vias are required. Vias are tented rather than plugged. Rev C uses four layers for routing room around the RP2354B and an inner ground reference. It supersedes earlier releases. All copper is generated by tscircuit's built-in local routing phases, with caches disabled.

CPL units are millimetres. Its origin is the PCB centre, matching the Gerbers; negative coordinates are intentional. Positions use footprint origins. The USB connector's asymmetric land-pattern bounding-box centre is not the placement origin. Supplier-aware exported rotations are preserved.

The six probe pads, four mounting holes, three fiducials and display glass are excluded from the assembly BOM. All 76 reference names are on the top component-side legend; the top assembly drawing identifies their positions.

Check component availability and the assembly preview during JLCPCB quoting, including RP2354B, USB connector, FPC connector, buck regulator and diode orientation. The supplied SKUs identify exact imported parts; their live stock is not guaranteed by the design files.

Purchase the **Good Display GDEY075T7** raw panel separately: 7.5-inch monochrome, 800 × 480, UC8179, 24 contacts at 0.5 mm pitch. Insert the tail into J2 with exposed contacts facing the PCB. The panel's boost and reservoir circuitry is on this controller PCB.

Program the assembled RP2354B using the included `../firmware/releases/rp2354b/USB-Epaper-RevC-RP2354B-v1.1.0.uf2`. Hold BOOTSEL, press/release RESET, then copy the UF2 to the RP2350 drive. `../firmware/README.md` covers USB image sending and refresh behavior. The enclosure STL/STEP files and assembly instructions are in `../enclosure/`. Rev C has passed software fabrication checks; electrical bring-up, refresh performance and printed fit still require a physical prototype.

Sources: [panel specifications](https://www.good-display.com/product/396.html), [JLCPCB capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities).
