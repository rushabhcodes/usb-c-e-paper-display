# JLCPCB order settings — Rev A

Upload `JLCPCB-Gerbers-RevA.zip` for the PCB, then `BOM.csv` and `CPL.csv` for top-side assembly.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Board | 100 × 60 mm, two copper layers, 1.6 mm FR-4 |
| Stack | Standard two-layer FR-4; 1 oz copper |
| Finish / colours | ENIG, black solder mask, white legend |
| Via process | Through vias, 0.15 mm drill / 0.30 mm pad, tented both sides |
| Fine drilling | Select the 0.15 mm via option; this incurs an extra charge |
| Routing | 0.15 mm minimum traces; 0.15 mm routing clearance target, 0.10 mm fabrication floor |
| Outline | Routed, 2 mm corner radius; four 3.2 mm mounting holes |
| Assembly | Top side, 62 components; match the supplied LCSC/JLCPCB codes |
| Stencil | Top only, 0.10 mm recommended; `SMT-Stencil-RevA.zip` |

Both layers carry signals and power; the bottom also has a ground pour. No blind, buried or filled vias are required. Small vias are tented rather than plugged. This routing revision uses two layers and supersedes the earlier four-layer external-router release. All copper is generated by tscircuit's built-in local routing phases, with caches disabled.

CPL units are millimetres. Its origin is the PCB centre, matching the Gerbers; negative coordinates are intentional. Positions use footprint origins. The USB connector's asymmetric land-pattern bounding-box centre is not the placement origin. Supplier-aware exported rotations are preserved.

The six probe pads, four mounting holes, three fiducials and display glass are excluded from the assembly BOM. Component reference names are printed on the reverse side; the top assembly drawing identifies their positions.

Check component availability and the assembly preview during JLCPCB quoting, including RP2040, USB connector, FPC connector, flash and diode orientation. The supplied SKUs identify exact imported parts; their live stock is not guaranteed by the design files.

Purchase the **Good Display GDEY075T7** raw panel separately: 7.5-inch monochrome, 800 × 480, UC8179, 24 contacts at 0.5 mm pitch. Insert the tail into J2 with exposed contacts facing the PCB. The panel's boost and reservoir circuitry is on this controller PCB.

Program the assembled RP2040 using the included `../firmware/releases/USB-Epaper-RevA-v1.0.0.uf2`. Hold BOOTSEL, press/release RESET, then copy the UF2 to the RPI-RP2 drive. `../firmware/README.md` covers USB image sending and refresh behavior. The enclosure STL/STEP files and assembly instructions are in `../enclosure/`. Rev A has passed software fabrication checks; electrical bring-up, refresh performance and printed fit still require a physical prototype.

Sources: [panel specifications](https://www.good-display.com/product/396.html), [JLCPCB capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities).
