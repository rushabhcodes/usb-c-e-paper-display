# Rev C RP2354B hardware

U1 is Raspberry Pi RP2354B, JLCPCB C39843328, QFN-80 with exposed ground pad
81. It contains 2 MB stacked flash. The external W25Q16 is removed; U5 is now
the core regulator. Dedicated QMI pins 70–74 have no PCB loads. QSPI_SS pin 75
retains the 1 kΩ BOOTSEL switch isolation and a 10 kΩ pull-up to 3.3 V.
QSPI_IOVDD is 3.3 V, as required for the internal Winbond flash die.

## Core supply

The design follows **external core supply mode in Figure 24** of the RP2350
datasheet. VREG_AVDD and VREG_VIN remain powered from 3.3 V with local C33;
VREG_FB and VREG_PGND connect to ground; VREG_LX is open. Three local bypass
capacitors feed the three DVDD pins from VCORE. Every I/O/USB/ADC/flash supply
has its own bypass capacitor.

U5 is TI TLV62569DBVR (C141836), supplied from fused USB VSYS and enabled by
V3V3. L2 is Sunlord MWSA0402S-2R2MT (C408335), 2.2 µH, with 5 A typical
saturation current and 58 mΩ maximum DCR. C34 provides 10 µF input capacitance.
C35/C36 provide **2 × 22 µF** output capacitance: this is the TI stability-table
combination for outputs below 1.2 V. The 8.2 kΩ / 10 kΩ, 1% divider sets
`0.6 × (1 + 8.2/10) = 1.092 V`. Including the 1% reference and resistor
tolerances gives approximately 1.071–1.113 V, within the 1.05–1.16 V DVDD
range for nominal operation. The software audit independently checks the
physical pins, resistor values, capacitor SKUs and the tolerance calculation.

The separate buck avoids the orientation-sensitive Abracon inductor specified
for the on-chip switching regulator, which was not found in the JLCPCB catalogue
during part selection. It also avoids dissipating the core's voltage drop in
a linear regulator inside the 18 mm enclosure.

## Firmware and mechanics

The firmware targets RP2350B, Arm Cortex-M33, 150 MHz and 2 MB flash through
Arduino-Pico 6.4.0. The original GP8–GP14 display assignments and host protocol
are preserved. The Rev C UF2 is different from the archived RP2040 images.
The build verifies secure-Arm family 0xe48bff59, the ROM compatibility block,
flash addresses and byte equality with the compiler's binary.

The 100 × 60 mm PCB outline, mounting holes, left-side USB-C datum and raw-panel
FPC datum stay aligned with the 182.2 × 137.2 × 18 mm enclosure. The component
clearance audit loads every assembled supplier model against the rear floor.
System diagrams and the controller engineering drawing identify RP2354B.
